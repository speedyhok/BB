# Breaking Bad Albuquerque 3D City - Map & Landmarks Overview

Welcome to the **Breaking Bad 3D City of Albuquerque** game asset world, meticulously built in Blender 4.0 for game development.

---

## 1. World Scale & Coordinate System
- **Scale**: Standard Game Engine Scale ($1.0 \text{ unit} = 1.0 \text{ meter}$)
- **World Dimensions**: $1200\text{m} \times 1200\text{m}$ ($1.44 \text{ km}^2$ explorable world space)
- **Origin $(0, 0, 0)$**: Central Avenue / Route 66 Commercial Crossing
- **Orientation**:
  - **$+X$**: East (toward the Sandia Mountains)
  - **$-X$**: West (toward Downtown and West Mesa)
  - **$+Y$**: North (Northeast Heights & To'hajiilee Desert Mesa)
  - **$-Y$**: South (South Valley Industrial District & Rail Yards)
  - **$+Z$**: Up (Elevation / Altitude)

## 3. District Zoning Breakdown

### District 1: Northeast Heights (Residential)
- Spans $Y = 200\text{m}$ to $380\text{m}$, $X = -280\text{m}$ to $180\text{m}$.
- Paved residential avenues with sidewalks, street trees, mailboxes, and Southwestern ranch/pueblo homes.
- Contains **Walt's House**, **Jesse's House**, and neighborhood street loops.

### District 2: Central Avenue / Route 66 (Commercial Strip)
- Spans East-West from $X = -550\text{m}$ to $320\text{m}$ along $Y = 0\text{m}$.
- 4-lane arterial boulevard with double yellow lines, dashed white lanes, crosswalks, sodium-vapor streetlights, and traffic light signals.
- Contains **Los Pollos Hermanos**, **Saul Goodman's Plaza**, **A1A Carwash**, and **Crossroads Motel**.

### District 3: Downtown Albuquerque (Skyline Core)
- Spans $X = -480\text{m}$ to $-220\text{m}$, $Y = -120\text{m}$ to $140\text{m}$.
- High-density urban district with 9 high-rise and mid-rise office towers (20m to 80m tall), including Albuquerque Plaza, Bernalillo County Courthouse, and bank headquarters.

### District 4: South Valley & Rail Yards (Industrial)
- Spans $Y = -150\text{m}$ to $-450\text{m}$, $X = -350\text{m}$ to $280\text{m}$.
- Heavy industrial zone with freight rail corridors, commercial distribution warehouses, chemical storage facilities, **Lavandería Brillante**, and **Old Joe's Junkyard**.

### District 5: Sandia Mountain Range (East Horizon)
- Spans $X = 340\text{m}$ to $620\text{m}$, rising dramatically from 0m foothills to 115m granite peaks.
- Forms an authentic natural eastern barrier for the city map with canyon cuts, rocky crags, and desert vegetation.

### District 6: To'hajiilee Desert Mesa (Northwest Outskirts)
- Spans $X = -550\text{m}$ to $-350\text{m}$, $Y = 250\text{m}$ to $550\text{m}$.
- Rugged red sandstone mesas and canyons accessed via an unpaved desert dirt road. Houses the secluded Crystal Ship RV cook site.
